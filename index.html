Our Story

A second
home.

Tommy's Original is the only barbershop in South Jersey that brings back that old-school communal feel — the kind where you're not just getting a cut, you're hanging out. You come in, your boys are already there, the conversation is flowing, and nobody's in a rush to leave. That energy that used to define every neighborhood barbershop, It lives here.

Walk through the door and you'll know exactly who Tommy is. The walls tell the story — Eagles, Sixers, Phillies, Flyers, Philly boxing legends — his Italian-American roots woven right alongside it all. It's personal. It's proud. And it makes the shop feel like somewhere you actually belong.

Modern cuts. Classic atmosphere. The kind of place your grandfather would've loved and you can't stop coming back to.

Our Neighborhood

Rooted in
Haddonfield.

Tommy's Original didn't land here by accident. Haddonfield, NJ has been a tight-knit community for centuries — a place where local businesses aren't just tolerated, they're the backbone of the town. We're proud to be part of that fabric.

From the tree-lined streets of Kings Highway to the familiar faces on Kings Court, this borough has a character all its own. Historic, walkable, full of life — and full of people who appreciate something done right.

We're not just a shop in Haddonfield. We're a Haddonfield shop. There's a difference.

F.A.Q's

Everything you need to know before your first visit — or your hundredth.

Yes — walk-ins are always welcome at Tommy's Original. That said, if you want to guarantee your spot at a specific time, booking an appointment is the way to go. We recommend calling ahead during peak hours (weekends especially).

You can book directly through our website using the "Quick Book Now" button, or give us a call. We also accept DMs on Instagram if that's easier for you. Appointments are held for 10 minutes, so try to be on time!

We're open Tuesday through Friday from 9am to 7pm, Saturday from 8am to 5pm, and Sunday from 10am to 3pm. We're closed on Mondays. Hours may vary on holidays so check our Instagram for updates.

Prices vary by service and barber. A standard cut starts at $30, with fades, beard work, and specialty services priced accordingly. Check our Services page for the full menu, or ask your barber at the time of your visit.

Absolutely. We love cutting kids' hair and our barbers are great with younger clients. We recommend booking an appointment for kids to minimize wait time and keep things stress-free for everyone.

Yes, and we encourage it. When booking, simply select the barber you'd like. Each barber has their own schedule so availability may vary — but we'll always do our best to get you with your preferred person.

We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, and Cash App. Tips are always appreciated and can be given in any of these forms as well.

Yes — beard shaping, trims, and lineup are all part of what we do. You can book a beard service on its own or add it on to your haircut. Ask your barber what they'd recommend for your specific beard type and goals.

We're located in downtown Haddonfield, NJ. Street parking is available on Kings Highway and surrounding streets. The downtown area is also very walkable, so if you're nearby you can always stroll over.

Your satisfaction matters to us. If something isn't right, let us know before you leave and your barber will make it right on the spot. We stand behind our work — no awkward conversations needed, just let us fix it.
